I am a final-year Ph.D. Student at [Southeast University (SEU)](https://www.seu.edu.cn/). Besides, I am also a Visiting student at Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) [AIR Lab](https://theairlab.org/), advised by Prof. [Sebastian Scherer](https://theairlab.org/team/sebastian/) and Prof. [Wenshan Wang](https://theairlab.org/team/wenshan/). I am currently collaborating with [Shibo Zhao](https://shibowing.github.io/) and [Yuheng Qiu](http://yuhengqiu.com/) at CMU RI. I have close collaborations with Prof. [Xingxing Zuo](https://xingxingzuo.github.io/) at MBZUAI, [Chen Wang](https://sairlab.org/chenw/) at Buffalo University and [Yuzhang Shang](https://42shawn.github.io/) at University of Central Florida (UCF). I greatly enjoyed my previous internship at Meituan Vision Intelligence Department, where I focused on 3D visual perception. During this internship, I was supervised by [Xiangxiang Chu](https://cxxgtxy.github.io/) and [Zhi Tian](https://zhitian.xyz/), and I also received valuable guidance from [Xinyu Zhang](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=zGLVABAAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN) and [Liang Li](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=z_fYeJoAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN). After this, I was fortunate to intern at Houmo.AI, concentrating on efficient AI, under the supervision of [Zhihang Yuan](https://zhihang.cc/). I earned my Master of Robotics from Northeastern University in 2021, advised by Prof. [Zheng Fang](http://faculty.neu.edu.cn/fangzheng/zh_CN/index.htm).

My research interests focus on **Efficient AI** (model compression/quantization for LLMs/MLLMs), **Spatial AI** (learning-based multi-sensor [LiDAR/Camera/IMU] perception for robotics), and **Embodied AI** (VLA/VLN for robotics).

My long-term research vision is to enable natural interaction and seamless collaboration between robots and humans in open environments by accurately understanding/reasoning robot states, surrounding 3D scenes, and action execution.
